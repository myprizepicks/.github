Why it hurts

The balance-sheet problem

  1. Liability is booked on credit. Promo funds sit until spent or manually expired — a recurring ops tax.
  2. Two products share one ledger. High-volume acquisition credits sit next to $30k–$50k discretionary VIP dumps.
  3. Campaign spikes linger. July referrals alone were $2.56M; unused balances remain on the books.
  4. Audit trail is thin. “Promo Balance Adjustment – Other” is $1.73M; bulk uploads lack structured reasons.

Recommended path

Replace promo funds with bonus lineups

Users get a promotional entry. Credit pays out only if the lineup wins. Losses and unused entries expire with the contest — no wallet residue, no manual zero-out campaigns.

Today

  • Liability created on grant
  • Manual / resource-heavy expiry
  • Free wallet balance mental model
  • Bulk upload + admin adjust ops

Target

  • Liability on win settlement only
  • Inherent contest expiry
  • Free shot that pays if it hits
  • Campaign config + auto settlement

Recommended path: replace with bonus lineups

Upfront promo-wallet grants create durable liability and a manual expiry burden. Bonus lineup promotions pay credit only if the lineup wins and expire with the contest — no residual wallet balance.

  1. Align & measure — Finance recognition, success metrics, inventory outstanding balances.
  2. Ship bonus lineup default — Configurable eligibility, distinct ledger, fraud caps.
  3. Remap channels — Referrals, NMO, deposit matches, bulk, VIP → bonus lineups or kill.
  4. Sunset with ramp — Freeze net-new wallet grants → holdouts → one automated wind-down.
  5. Govern — Dual approval above threshold; CRM language shifts to “bonus lineups.”
